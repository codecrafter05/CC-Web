Custom E-commerce Development

Code Crafter builds e-commerce platforms in Bahrain that are fast, secure, and tailored to your business — not adapted from a template. Whether you need a luxury product store, a multi-vendor marketplace, or a subscription-based shop, we build it from scratch with clean code that scales.

We've built Al Jiwan Jewellery — a luxury e-commerce store with multi-currency and multi-payment support — and Bahar, a full marketplace app bringing together Bahrain's shops on iOS and Android. We also built Zoes, a subscription-based booking and service platform. Our e-commerce work proves we can handle everything from elegant product presentation to complex payment integrations.

What We Build

  • Single-brand e-commerce stores
  • Multi-vendor marketplace platforms
  • Subscription & membership shops
  • Luxury and fashion e-commerce
  • Food & restaurant ordering systems
  • B2B wholesale and trade platforms
  • Mobile e-commerce apps (iOS & Android)
