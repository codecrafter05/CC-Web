Road2Pro Academy is a professional gaming and esports training platform in Bahrain, connecting players with coaches for consultation sessions. The platform handles booking, payment, and training schedule management — giving aspiring esports professionals a structured path to improvement.

Road2Pro — Esports Training Platform

The Challenge

The esports coaching industry in Bahrain lacked a professional, structured platform. Road2Pro needed a system that could match players with qualified coaches, manage session bookings, process payments, and maintain training records — all while keeping the experience simple for users who expect the same quality they see in global gaming platforms. The platform also needed to handle varying session types, availability windows, and coach profiles.

Key Features

What We Built

The platform includes coach profiles with specializations and availability, a consultation booking system with real-time slot management, secure online payment processing, and a training management dashboard. Both coaches and students have dedicated dashboards — coaches manage their schedule and sessions, while students track their progress and upcoming bookings. The system is built to scale as Road2Pro grows its coach roster and player base across Bahrain.

Result
