Bahar is a flagship marketplace platform that brings together Bahrain's shops in one place — available on both iOS and Android. It's not just a shopping app: it's a complete ecosystem with delivery management, installment payments via Floos, a POS system, and a full merchant dashboard that scales for any business size.

Bahar — Full Marketplace Platform

The Challenge

Building a marketplace in Bahrain that could genuinely compete with regional giants required solving multiple complex problems at once. Merchants needed tools to manage their products, orders, and customers — but those tools had to work for a small boutique and a large retailer equally. Shoppers needed a seamless discovery and checkout experience. Delivery had to be coordinated with third-party logistics. And the payment layer had to support both standard card transactions and installment-based buying through the Floos platform. Above all, it had to work flawlessly on both iOS and Android with native-quality performance.

What We Built

Platform Features

Bahar is built on a multi-layered architecture that serves shoppers, merchants, and delivery partners simultaneously. The shopper app (iOS and Android) provides product discovery, secure checkout, order tracking, and Floos installment payment integration. The merchant dashboard allows any business — from a single-person shop to a large retailer — to manage products, inventory, orders, and analytics from one place. The built-in POS system extends Bahar's reach to physical stores, unifying online and offline sales. Delivery companies are integrated directly, so logistics are managed within the same ecosystem rather than through separate tools.

02

Technology

Built With the Latest Stack

Bahar is built using the latest cross-platform mobile technology for native-quality iOS and Android performance, backed by a scalable Laravel API. The platform integrates with Floos for installment payments, local delivery APIs, and a real-time order management system. The entire codebase is written from scratch — no templates, no shortcuts — ensuring maintainability and performance at scale. Available on the App Store and Google Play.

A Complete Ecosystem
