Website Cost in Bahrain — 2026

June 2026 Web Development8 min read

One of the most common questions we get from businesses in Bahrain: "How much does a website cost?" The honest answer is — it depends entirely on what you need. But that answer frustrates people, so here's a real breakdown of what businesses in Bahrain actually pay in 2026, and what drives the price in each direction.

1. Simple Brochure Website — BHD 200 to 600

A simple website with 4–6 pages (Home, About, Services, Contact) built with a template or page builder typically costs between BHD 200–600. This is suitable for freelancers, small shops, or businesses that just need an online presence to share their address, phone number, and a brief description of what they do.

What you get: A professional-looking site, mobile-responsive, basic SEO setup, contact form.

What you don't get: Anything custom. You're constrained by the template's design, and updates will often require the developer's help.

2. Business / Corporate Website — BHD 600 to 2,500

A proper corporate website — with a custom design, multiple sections, bilingual content (Arabic + English), animations, and a clean admin panel — typically falls in the BHD 600–2,500 range. This is what most established businesses in Bahrain should invest in.

For context: our project Strategic Marketing is a bilingual corporate website in this category. At this level, your website reflects your brand properly, ranks better on Google, and leaves a strong impression on visitors.

3. E-commerce Website — BHD 1,500 to 5,000+

Building an online store is significantly more complex. You need product management, a shopping cart, secure checkout, payment gateway integration, order management, and often inventory tracking. A well-built e-commerce site in Bahrain starts at BHD 1,500 and can go much higher depending on complexity.

Our project Al Jiwan Jewellery is a luxury e-commerce store with multi-currency support, multiple payment methods, and a sophisticated product browsing experience. That's the upper end of this range — because it required a level of polish that matches the brand's positioning.

4. Custom Platform / Web Application — BHD 3,000+

If you need a booking system, a marketplace, an LMS, an ERP, or any application where users have accounts and interact with data — you're in the custom platform category. These are full software products, not websites. Pricing starts at BHD 3,000 and easily reaches BHD 10,000–20,000 for complex systems.

Examples from our portfolio: Zoes (salon booking + subscriptions), Al Hadh Al Wafer (LMS with exam system), Bahar (full marketplace app on iOS and Android). These aren't websites — they're systems that run a business.

What Drives the Price Up?

  • Bilingual (Arabic + English): Doubles content work and requires proper RTL support
  • Custom design from scratch: Adds BHD 200–800 vs. using a template
  • Payment integration: Every gateway (Benefit, Stripe, Floos) adds setup time
  • Third-party APIs: WhatsApp notifications, delivery tracking, etc.
  • Admin panels and dashboards: These are full applications inside the application
  • Ongoing maintenance: A proper website needs updates, security patches, and hosting

Template vs. Custom Code — Which Should You Choose?

Templates are fine for simple businesses with simple needs. But if you have specific business requirements, need to stand out from competitors, or plan to grow — custom code is the better investment. Templates constrain you. Custom code scales with you.
